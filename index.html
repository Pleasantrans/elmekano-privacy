Politik konfidansyalite

El Mekano, yon jwèt Altenativ Group · Dènye mizajou: 29 oktòb 2026

El Mekano se yon jwèt 3D ki aprann timoun mekanik machin, moto, kamyon ak bis. Paj sa a di ki enfòmasyon jwèt la itilize ak ki sa nou fè avèk yo.

Done nou pa kolekte

Jwèt la pa kolekte, pa sove sou yon sèvè, pa vann ni pa pataje okenn enfòmasyon pèsonèl. Li pa mande non, laj, imèl, nimewo telefòn, kont, foto, mikwofòn, kamera ni lokalizasyon.

  • Pa gen kont oswa login.
  • Pa gen piblisite.
  • Pa gen achte andedan jwèt la.
  • Pa gen analitik ni swivi konpòtman.
  • Pa gen chat ni kominikasyon ak lòt moun.

Sa ki rete sou aparèy la

Jwèt la sove sèlman sou telefòn oswa tablèt la: zetwal ou genyen, nivo ou, estatistik konpetans, ak si son an koupe. Done sa yo pa kite aparèy la. Ou ka efase yo nenpòt ki lè ak bouton "reset" nan menu a, nan Paramèt Android (Aplikasyon → El Mekano → Stokaj → Efase done), oswa lè w dezenstale jwèt la.

Timoun

Jwèt la fèt pou timoun 5 a 15 an. Paske li pa kolekte okenn enfòmasyon pèsonèl, paran yo pa bezwen ba nou anyen pou timoun nan jwe.

Chanjman

Si jwèt la chanje (pa egzanp si nou ajoute yon fonksyon ki bezwen done), nou ap mete paj sa a ajou anvan, epi nou ap chanje dat mizajou a.

Privacy policy

El Mekano, a game by Altenativ Group · Last updated: October 29, 2026

El Mekano is a 3D game that teaches children how cars, motorcycles, trucks and buses are repaired. This page explains what information the game uses and what we do with it.

Data we do not collect

The game does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information. It does not ask for a name, age, email, phone number, account, photos, microphone, camera or location.

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No ads.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No analytics or behavior tracking.
  • No chat or communication with other people.

What stays on the device

The game saves only on the phone or tablet: your stars, rank, skill statistics, and whether sound is muted. This data never leaves the device. You can erase it at any time with the reset button in the menu, in Android Settings (Apps → El Mekano → Storage → Clear data), or by uninstalling the game.

Children

The game is designed for children aged 5 to 15. Because it collects no personal information, parents do not need to provide anything for a child to play.

Changes

If the game changes in a way that involves data (for example, a new feature that needs it), we will update this page first and change the date above.
